A Somali aviation brand built for the world.

Bilan Air is a Somali aviation brand built to deliver safe, reliable, and high-quality air travel across Africa and beyond. We combine international aviation standards with a strong Somali identity to create a trusted and respected brand.

We believe that great aviation is about more than getting from A to B. It's about the experience, the people, and the story. Every flight we operate carries Somali pride, and every passenger we serve is treated as family.

From our dedicated ground teams to our carefully trained cabin crew, every person at Bilan Air is united by one vision: to make Somali aviation something the world looks up to.

Our Journey

From idea to airline

2021

Founded in Nairobi

Bilan Air is incorporated by a team of Somali aviation professionals with a shared vision: a world-class airline representing Somalia.

2022

First Route: Nairobi – Mogadishu

We launch our inaugural route, operating daily scheduled services between Wilson Airport and Aden Adde International Airport.

2023

Fleet Expansion & Cargo Launch

Second aircraft joins the fleet. Bilan Air launches dedicated cargo services supporting East African trade corridors.

2024

50,000 Passengers Milestone

We reach 50,000 passengers carried, a proud moment for our team and a testament to the trust placed in us by the Somali community.

2025

Regional Expansion: 6 Countries

New routes to Djibouti, Addis Ababa, and Dar es Salaam. Bilan Air now serves 6 countries across East Africa.

2026

Beyond East Africa

Long-haul planning underway. Bilan Air sets its sights on connecting the Somali diaspora in Europe and the Gulf with direct routes.
