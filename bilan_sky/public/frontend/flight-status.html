Flight delays

If your flight is delayed by more than 3 hours, you are entitled to a meal voucher and complimentary refreshments at the gate. Contact our ground team for assistance.

Checked baggage

Bags are typically available at the carousel within 20–30 minutes of landing. If your bag has not arrived after 45 minutes, please visit the Bilan Air baggage desk.

Flight disruptions

For cancellations or major disruptions, our customer care team is reachable 24/7 on +254 700 245 100 or via WhatsApp at the same number.
